Church Membership

View on the current site
How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.

Introduction

Becoming part of a local church is a vital step in your walk with Christ. You were not meant to follow Jesus alone!

God’s Design For Community

Hebrews 10:24-25 "Let us consider how we may one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up together, as some are in the habit of doing, but encouraging one another."

Ephesians 2:19 "You are no longer foreigners and strangers, but fellow with God’s people and also members of His ."

The Church is the Body of Christ

1 Corinthians 12:27 "Now you are the of Christ, and each one of you is a of it."

Romans 12:5
"In Christ we, though many, form one , and each member belongs to all the others.”

We each have a part to play in the church. Church membership is not about attending a building—it’s about belonging, growing, and serving in God’s family.

Why Membership Matters

  • It reflects your commitment to spiritual growth
  • It gives you a place to be encouraged and held accountable
  • It opens the door to use your gifts to serve others
  • It places you under spiritual leadership and care

Joining a Local Church

Have you joined a local church yet? Yes No

If not, what is holding you back?

Action Step: Talk to a pastor or leader about becoming a member. Ask what steps are required and how you can get connected.
