Look up. Look in. Look out.

Three simple directions that cover a whole life with God. Do them in order, one lesson a week, one on one or in a small group.

↑Look Up

Knowing, loving and walking with the Lord. Word, worship, prayer, listening, abiding.

  • Vision for a Vibrant Relationship
  • Devoted to God's Word
  • Devoted to Worship
  • Devoted to Prayer
  • Devoted to Listening
  • Devoted to Abiding and Obeying
↓Look In

Holiness, love and Christlike character. Confess, repent, replace, restore.

  • Our New Life in Christ
  • Confession: Agreeing with God's Truth
  • Repentance: Turning Away from Sin
  • Replacement: Weeding and Feeding
  • Restoration: Holiness and Love
→Look Out

A Great Commission lifestyle. Your identity, your people, your story, your disciples.

  • Your Identity as a Witness and Ambassador
  • Your Sphere of Influence
  • Sharing the Gospel with Confidence
  • Making Disciples Who Make Disciples

Questions we hear a lot

Who should I disciple?
Start with who God has already put around you, and look for someone who's hungry. Read the guide.
Do I have to be an expert?
Nope. Every lesson tells you what to read, what to ask, and what to share. Your story is the part only you can bring.
One on one or a group?
Either works. The lessons were written for both.
Is there a map of the whole thing?
Yep. Download the Discipleship Roadmap and keep it in your Bible.
Where can I learn more about this approach?
Watch our Life on Life Discipleship teaching series.
I'm not at Antioch. Can I use these?
Please do. That's the whole point.
