Devoted to Abiding and Obeying

1Connection2Vision3Biblical Foundations4Modeling5Practice

Devoted to Abiding and Obeying · Look Up · Lesson 6 of 6 · discipleshiplessons.com

How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.
1

Connection

As you get started, catch up on how y’all are doing since you last met. Celebrate how God has been at work in your lives. Ask how have the 40 Days of Devotion gone so far. Share something meaningful from your times with the Lord.

2

Vision

Jesus wants us to keep in step with the Spirit, obeying His leadership and enjoying our ongoing communion with Him. As we walk with Him we walk in His life and peace.

3

Questions for Discussion:

  1. What gets you excited about walking with Jesus throughout the day?
  2. What are the biggest obstacles to "abiding" throughout your day?
  3. In what area of your life is God currently calling you to greater obedience?
4

Modeling

For the Leader: As the discipleship leader, share your own experience with obeying and abiding in Christ throughout the day. Describe practical ways you remind yourself of God's presence, how you respond to His promptings in real-time, and how this has affected your daily life.

5

Practice

We want to practice abiding in Christ and walking in obedience throughout our day. As part of the 40 Days of Devotion, you'll be guided through practices that help you extend your devotional life into your entire day.
