Sharing the Gospel with Confidence

1Connection2Vision3Biblical Foundations4Modeling5Practice

Sharing the Gospel with Confidence · Look Out · Lesson 3 of 4 · discipleshiplessons.com

How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.
1

Connection

As you get started, catch up on how y’all are doing since you last met. Celebrate how God has been at work in your lives. How did it go praying for those in your sphere of influence?

2

Vision

The gospel is the power of God for salvation. That means the power isn’t in how well you say it. It’s in the message itself. Your part is to share it clearly and with love, and trust God with the results.

3

Biblical Foundations

Romans 1:16 "For I am not of the gospel, because it is the of God that brings salvation to everyone who believes: first to the Jew, then to the Gentile."

1 Corinthians 15:3-5 "For what I received I passed on to you as of importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures, and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve."

Acts 4:29-31 "Now, Lord, consider their threats and enable your servants to speak your word with great . Stretch out your hand to heal and perform signs and wonders through the name of your holy servant Jesus. After they prayed, the place where they were meeting was shaken. And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit and spoke the word of God ."

Questions for Discussion:

  1. Why do you think Paul said he was "not ashamed" of the gospel?
  2. What gives you confidence in sharing the gospel message?
  3. What aspects of sharing your faith feel most challenging?
4

Modeling

For the Leader: Model sharing the gospel in a clear, conversational way. Show how you would share the core message in about 3-5 minutes. Then explain why you chose to emphasize certain aspects and how you try to contextualize the message for different audiences while keeping the core truth intact.

Together: Go out together and pray for people, and share the Gospel. A good rule of thumb is for you and the person you are discipling to each share at least three times with people.

5

Practice

This week make it a goal to share with three people. This might come through praying for them, sharing your testimony or sharing the Gospel.

Remember: Success in evangelism is faithfully sharing the message and leaving the results to God. Our job is to plant and water seeds; God brings the growth (1 Corinthians 3:6-7).
