The Gospel

View on the current site
How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.

Introduction

This lesson walks through the basic truths of the Gospel and gives people an opportunity to respond to Jesus. You can also use Billy Graham’s Steps to Peace with God tract for this lesson.

Step 1: God's Purpose - Peace and Life

God loves you and wants you to experience peace and life—abundant and eternal.

John 3:16 "For God so the world that He gave His one and only , that whoever in Him shall not perish but have life."

John 10:10 (Jesus speaking) "I have come that they may have , and that they may have it more ."

Question: What does God want for your life according to these verses?

Step 2: Our Problem - Sin

God created us to have fellowship with Him, but we chose to go our own way, which broke our relationship with God. This sin leads to death and eternal separation from God.

Romans 3:23 "For have sinned and fall of the glory of God."

Romans 6:23 "For the wages of sin is , but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord."

Think about it: Sin is not just doing bad things; it is rejecting God and His good and perfect leadership for our lives and choosing to go our own way.

How have you seen sin cause separation in your relationship with God and with others relationships?

Step 3: God's Remedy — The Cross

God sent His Son Jesus Christ to die on the cross for our sins—taking the punishment we deserved.

Romans 5:8 "But God demonstrates His own toward us, in that while we were still , Christ died for us."

1 Peter 3:18 "For Christ also suffered once for sins, the for the , to bring you to God."

Jesus' death demonstrates God’s love for us, ransoms us from our sin and restores us to God.

Step 4: Our Response — Receive Christ

We must individually receive Jesus Christ as Savior and Lord.

John 1:12 "Yet to all who did Him, to those who in His name, He gave the right to become children of God."

Romans 10:9-10 "If you with your mouth, 'Jesus is Lord,' and in your heart that God raised Him from the dead, you will be saved. For it is with your heart that you believe and are justified, and it is with your mouth that you profess your faith and are saved."

Ephesians 2:8-9 "For it is by you have been saved, through — and this is not from yourselves, it is the of God — not by , so that no one can boast."

Your Decision

Have you received Jesus Christ as your Lord and Savior? Do you commit to following Him all of your days?

Yes No

If you are not sure, you can do it by praying this with all your heart:

"Lord Jesus, I need You. I acknowledge that I have sinned and separated myself from You. Thank You for dying on the cross for my sins. I believe You rose from the dead. I now turn from my sin and invite You to come into my life. I receive You as my personal Savior and Lord. Thank You for forgiving my sins and giving me eternal life. Help me to follow You from this day forward. Amen."

On / / I received Jesus in my heart to be my Savior and I have committed to follow Him. I have become a child of God, and His Spirit lives inside me.
