Discipleship Foundations

View on the current site

Jesus calls us all to go and make disciples (Matthew 28), but sometimes it can be difficult to know where to start.

Discipleship Foundations is a simple but powerful set of lessons and experiences to lay good foundations in the person you are investing in.

These lessons can be done in a one on one fashion, or in a discipleship group. Along with the lessons, your Zone Pastors, Section Leaders or Lifegroup Leaders would love to help you get off on a strong foot in making disciples, so don’t hesitate to reach out to them!

Foundations for Seekers

Preview: This discipleship lesson is designed to be done with non-Christians who are interested in Jesus. In it, you will read through the Gospel of John together and learn more about Him.

Lesson:

  • Gospel of John Bible Study

Foundations for New Believers

Preview: This set of discipleship lessons is designed to be done with new believers. In it, you will focus on the Gospel, baptism, deliverance and church membership.

Lessons:

  • Gospel Presentation
  • Your Testimony
  • Water Baptism
  • Deliverance Prayer
  • Baptism of the Holy Spirit
  • Church Membership

Experiences: Baptism, Church Membership

Foundations for Growing Disciples

Part 1: Look Up

Preview: This set of discipleship lessons are designed to be done with a growing disciple and lays the foundation for developing a lifestyle of knowing, loving and walking with the Lord.

Lessons:

  • Vision For A Vibrant Relationship with the Lord
  • Devoted to God’s Word
  • Devoted to Worship
  • Devoted to Prayer
  • Devoted to Listening
  • Devoted to Abiding and Obeying

Experiences in Look Up: 40 Days of Devotion

Part 2: Look In

Preview: This set of discipleship lessons are designed to be done with a growing disciple after completing Look Up. Look In lays the foundations for a lifestyle of holiness, love and Christlike character.

Lessons:

  • Our New Life in Christ
  • Confession - Agreeing with God's Truth
  • Repentance - Turning Away from Sin
  • Replacement - Weeding and Feeding
  • Restoration - Living in Holiness and Love

Experiences in: Freedom Prayer Experience

Part 3: Look Out

Preview: This set of discipleship lessons are designed to be done with a growing disciple after completing Look Up and Look In. Look Out lays the foundation for a Great Commission lifestyle.

Lessons:

  • Your Identity as a Witness and Ambassador
  • Your Sphere of Influence
  • Sharing the Gospel with Confidence
  • Making Disciples Who Make Disciples
