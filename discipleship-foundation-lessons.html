Discipleship Foundations

15 lessons + a planning guide · 4 parts · about 4 months

For a believer who wants to grow.

Fifteen lessons that build a lifestyle of knowing, loving and walking with Jesus. Do the parts in order, then plan your next season together.

Part 1 · Look UpThese lessons focus on growing a lifestyle of loving Jesus and walking with Him daily. Practically, this unit will help the person you are discipling start, strengthen, or reignite the daily habit of spending time with the Lord.

1Vision for a Vibrant Relationship with the Lord2Devoted to God's Word3Devoted to Worship4Devoted to Prayer5Devoted to Listening to the Holy Spirit6Devoted to Abiding and ObeyingExperience40 Days of DevotionJoin →

Part 2 · Look InThese lessons focus on growing a lifestyle of holiness, love, and Christlike character. Practically, this unit will help the person you are discipling walk through a freedom experience of confessing sin, finding healing, and being filled with the Holy Spirit and the truth of God’s Word.

1Growing Into Our New Life in Christ2Confession3Repentance4Replace5RestoreExperienceFreedom Prayer ExperienceOpen →

Part 3 · Look OutThese lessons focus on growing a Great Commission lifestyle. Practically, this unit will help the person you are discipling practice sharing the Gospel with others and become a disciplemaker.

1Your Identity as a Witness and Ambassador2Your Sphere of Influence3Sharing the Gospel with Confidence4Making Disciples Who Make DisciplesExperienceShort-Term Mission TripSee trips →

Part 4 · Further GrowthFor when you finish Look Out: bearing fruit in every good work and growing in the knowledge of God. Practically, this guide will help you and the person you are discipling plan your next season together with good questions, books and practices.
