Tending Your Heart

Tending Your Heart · Further Growth · discipleshiplessons.com

These four short videos and worksheets are designed to help you build a richer relationship with the Lord through listening prayer. Watch one together each time you meet, then work through its worksheet.

1The Father’s Invitation to Intimacy

Jesus died so we could enjoy two-way conversation with God. Learn how Jesus lived listening to the Father, and a simple test for recognizing God’s voice.

Watch lesson 1 on YouTubeWorksheet for lesson 1 (PDF)

2Hindrances to Hearing God

What gets in the way of listening, and how to prepare your heart: worship, surrender, quieting distractions, asking simple questions and keeping a listening journal.

Watch lesson 2 on YouTubeWorksheet for lesson 2 (PDF)

3Overcoming Wounds and Lies

Lies are often sown in wounds. Walk through forgiving those who hurt you, renouncing the lies, and receiving God’s truth in their place.

Watch lesson 3 on YouTubeWorksheet for lesson 3 (PDF)

4How to Tend Your Heart

Keep talking with God through the day. When something triggers you, ask Him what it’s really about, and receive what He has for you in exchange.

Watch lesson 4 on YouTubeWorksheet for lesson 4 (PDF)
