Going Deeper

Plan Your Next Season of Discipleship

View on the current site

Introduction

This guide is designed to help you prayerfully plan your next season for a discipleship group you are leading or relationship you are investing in. It is designed to be done after your group members have been through our discipleship foundations lessons.

Questions For Prayer

Here are 6 questions to help you prayerfully plan your next season of a discipleship group:

1. Lord, who should I invite to be a part of the discipleship group in this season ahead?

2. Lord, what do You want to do in this discipleship group in this season?

3. How would You want us to focus and grow in Look Up, Look In, Look Out in the season ahead?Are there any specific needs, activities or resources You want us to focus on?

4. How do you want me to pray for my group members in the season ahead?

5. What is our church doing in this season and how can our group be a part?

6. Who in this group do I need to be training to lead their own discipleship group?

Using these 6 questions at the start of any season, to put together a simple plan for your group or discipleship relationship!
