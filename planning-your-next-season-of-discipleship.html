1

Introduction

This guide is designed to help you prayerfully plan your next season for a discipleship group you are leading or relationship you are investing in. It is designed to be done after your group members have been through our discipleship foundations lessons.

2

Questions For Prayer

Here are 6 questions to help you prayerfully plan your next season with a discipleship group:

  1. Lord, who should I invite to be a part of the discipleship group in this season ahead?
  2. Lord, what do You want to do in this discipleship group in this season?
  3. How would You want us to focus and grow in Look Up, Look In, Look Out in the season ahead? Are there any specific needs, activities or resources You want us to focus on?
  4. How do you want me to pray for my group members in the season ahead?
  5. What is our church doing in this season and how can our group be a part?
  6. Who in this group do I need to be training to lead their own discipleship group?

Use these 6 questions at the start of any season to put together a simple plan for your group or discipleship relationship!

3

Discipleship Questions

Use these questions when you meet in the season ahead. Pick a few from each area that fit where the person you are discipling is right now.

Look Up

Developing a lifestyle of seeking, knowing, loving, and walking with God

  • How has your time with Jesus been lately?
  • What are 3 words you’d use to describe your relationship with God?
  • Have you felt close to God or distant from Him lately? Why?
  • Is God asking you to take a step of obedience in any area right now?
  • What has God been teaching you recently through Scripture, a sermon, or life experience?
  • Have you been spending time in God’s Word? If so, what have you been reading?
  • What has stood out to you in Scripture recently?
  • How are you applying what you’re reading to your daily life?
  • How can you be more intentional in reading the Bible this week?
  • In what ways are you satisfied or dissatisfied with your prayer life right now?
  • What is something you’ve been consistently praying for?
  • How are you abiding in Jesus throughout the day?

Look In

Cultivating a lifestyle of holiness, love, and Christlike character

  • Has the Holy Spirit been convicting you of anything lately?
  • What burdens, needs, challenges, or big decisions can I/we pray with you about?
  • Is there any sin you need to confess and repent of?
  • In what ways are you seeing the fruit of the Spirit (Galatians 5:22–23) growing in your life?
  • Are there any areas in your life where you’re struggling to trust God?
  • Where do you feel a need to surrender control to God right now?
  • Where have you seen God at work in your life recently?
  • Have you cared more about what God thinks about your decisions recently, or what your friends think?
  • When you think about what matters most, are you happy with how you’re managing your time, or is there anything you’d like to change?

Look Out

Reflecting God’s heart to the people closest to you and living on mission

  • How can you love the people you live with more intentionally?
  • If I were to ask your family to describe how you’ve been this week, what would they say?
  • Are any of your friends or family hurting that you could encourage this week?
  • Would your friends describe you as someone who consistently points them toward Jesus? Why or why not?
  • Is there anyone you’ve been impatient, frustrated, or unkind with lately?
  • Are your closest friendships helping you become more like Jesus?
  • How do you respond when your friends are gossiping or talking negatively about someone?
  • Is there anyone you’ve been excluding or overlooking that God might be asking you to include?
  • What is one way you could make someone in your family feel loved this week?
  • Where is God asking you to put someone else’s needs ahead of your own?
  • Who in your life doesn’t know Jesus yet?
  • Who are you praying for or intentionally trying to share the Gospel with?
  • How are you being salt and light in your sphere of influence?
  • Are there any prodigals that you can be praying for and/or pursuing?
  • When someone in your life isn’t following Jesus, do you tend to feel compassion or judgment?
  • Who is someone you could offer to pray for this week?
  • How can you intentionally bring Jesus into the spaces you’re already in this week?
4

John Wesley’s 22 Questions

John Wesley and his friends used these 22 questions to cultivate a lifestyle of holiness and love.

  1. Am I consciously or unconsciously creating the impression that I am better than I really am? In other words, am I a hypocrite?
  2. Am I honest in all my acts and words, or do I exaggerate?
  3. Do I confidentially pass on to another what was told to me in confidence?
  4. Can I be trusted?
  5. Am I a slave to dress, friends, work, or habits?
  6. Am I self-conscious, self-pitying, or self-justifying?
  7. Did the Bible live in me today?
  8. Do I give it time to speak to me every day?
  9. Am I enjoying prayer?
  10. When did I last speak to someone else about my faith?
  11. Do I pray about the money I spend?
  12. Do I get to bed on time and get up on time?
  13. Do I disobey God in anything?
  14. Do I insist upon doing something about which my conscience is uneasy?
  15. Am I defeated in any part of my life?
  16. Am I jealous, impure, critical, irritable, touchy, or distrustful?
  17. How do I spend my spare time?
  18. Am I proud?
  19. Do I thank God that I am not as other people, especially as the Pharisees who despised the publican?
  20. Is there anyone whom I fear, dislike, disown, criticize, hold a resentment toward or disregard? If so, what am I doing about it?
  21. Do I grumble or complain constantly?
  22. Is Christ real to me?
