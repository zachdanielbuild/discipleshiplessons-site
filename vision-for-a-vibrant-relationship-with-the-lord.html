Vision for a Vibrant Relationship with the Lord

How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.

Introduction

As you get started, share vision for growing in your relationship with the Lord together and what you are excited about as you go through Look Up together.

Biblical Foundations

Jesus didn't just call His disciples to work for Him but first and foremost to be with Him. Even before sending them out to do ministry, He wanted them to experience His presence, learn from Him, and develop a relationship with Him.

The Scriptures use a variety of images to paint this picture including vines and branches, fountains of water, shepherds and sheep and the language of friendship and joy. Here are a few key Scriptures on this topic:

Questions for Discussion: What does it mean to "know" Jesus rather than just know about Him? What excites you about building this type of relationship with Jesus?

Modeling

For the Leader: One of the key habits for developing our relationship with the Lord is spending daily time in the Scriptures, prayer, worship and listening to him. We call this a personal devotional life. If we can help disciples build a daily habit of spending time with Jesus in His Word, in worship, in prayer and in listening we plant the seeds for a lifelong, life-giving friendship with the Lord.

Take time to model how you do your daily devotional life and explain how it your relationship with God and your daily life.
