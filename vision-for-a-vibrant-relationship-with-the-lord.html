Vision for a Vibrant Relationship with the Lord

1Connection2Vision3Biblical Foundations4Modeling5Practice

Vision for a Vibrant Relationship with the Lord · Look Up · Lesson 1 of 6 · discipleshiplessons.com

How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.
1

Connection

Catch up on each other’s week and pray together. Then share what you are excited about as you go through Look Up together and grow in your relationship with the Lord.

2

Vision

Share this with them in your own words:

Jesus didn’t call His disciples just to work for Him. He called them first and foremost to be with Him. Before He ever sent them out, He wanted them to know His presence, learn from Him and become His friends. That’s what He wants with you too: a real, growing, daily friendship.

3

Biblical Foundations

Questions for Discussion:

  1. Mark 3:14: What did Jesus call the twelve to do before He sent them out? Why do you think He put that first?
  2. John 15: What does Jesus promise will happen as we abide in Him? What does He say happens apart from Him?
  3. Psalm 16:11: What do we find in God’s presence? When have you experienced that?
  4. Philippians 3:8: How did Paul value knowing Jesus compared to everything else?
  5. What does it mean to “know” Jesus rather than just know about Him?
  6. What excites you about building this kind of relationship with Jesus?
4

Modeling

One of the key habits for growing in our relationship with the Lord is a personal devotional life: daily time with Jesus in His Word, worship, prayer, and listening. When we help someone build this habit, we plant the seeds for a lifelong, life-giving friendship with the Lord.

For the Leader: Share how you spend daily time with the Lord. Tell them when and where you meet with Him, what you do, and how it shapes your relationship with God and your day. Be honest about what is hard.

5

Practice

40 Days of Devotion is a daily email that helps you spend time with Jesus each day and keep abiding in Him through the day. Before you finish, sign up together and plan to share how it is going when you meet next.

Challenge:

  1. Sign up for 40 Days of Devotion
  2. Every day for the next 40 days, set aside focused time to “be with the Lord” and use the devotionals to help structure your daily times.
  3. Start a journal to record what God is showing you
