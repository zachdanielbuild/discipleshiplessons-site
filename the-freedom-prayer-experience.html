Freedom Prayer Experience

Freedom Prayer Experience · Freedom Prayer Experience · discipleshiplessons.com

Our freedom prayer experience is designed to help the person you are discipling encounter God and His healing and delivering power in their lives.

Each inventory addresses a practical area of their lives and gives opportunity to practice confession and repentance and to receive the cleansing ministry of the Holy Spirit empowering them for new levels of freedom and life in Christ.

Go through these one at a time over a period of weeks, or all together in an intensive or retreat type format.
