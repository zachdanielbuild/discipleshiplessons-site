Connection

As you get started, catch up on how y’all are doing since you last met. Celebrate how God has been at work in your lives.

This lesson walks through the basics of water baptism.

2

Vision

Water Baptism is an important step of obedience in your discipleship to Jesus.

3

Understanding Water Baptism

Matthew 28:19-20 "Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and them to obey everything I have commanded you."

Acts 2:41 "Those who accepted his message were , and about three thousand were added to their number that day."

4

What Baptism Means

Romans 6:3-4 "Or don't you know that all of us who were baptized into Christ Jesus were baptized into His ? We were therefore with Him through baptism into death in order that, just as Christ was raised from the dead through the glory of the Father, we too may live a life."

Baptism illustrates Christ's death, burial, and resurrection. When you are baptized, you are showing that you have died to your old life (going under the water) and have been raised to a new life in Christ (coming up out of the water).

5

Who Should Be Baptized?

Acts 8:36-38 "As they traveled along the road, they came to some water and the eunuch said, 'Look, here is water. What can stand in the way of ?' And he gave orders to stop the chariot. Then both Philip and the eunuch went down into the water and Philip him."

Acts 2:38 "Peter replied, ' and be , every one of you, in the name of Jesus Christ for the forgiveness of your sins.'"

Every person who has believed in Jesus Christ should be baptized. Baptism does not make you a believer; it demonstrates that you already are one. Baptism does not "save" you; only Jesus saves. Baptism is a sign that you are saved.
