We love celebrating stories of people in our church family making disciples! Here are some stories to celebrate and stir your faith!

1

Investing in Fathers

Alongside his roles as husband, father, and counselor, Bill serves as a Section Leader for our Young Families Lifegroups. In that role, he spends time meeting with, mentoring and coaching young fathers who are leading Lifegroups. Here's what those guys said about Bill: His availability and willingness to meet consistently created a big impact in their lives and their groups!

2

Making Disciples in Youth Ministry

In addition to his job as a coach and teacher, Kyle faithfully leads a high school guys’ Lifegroup. He is intentional to show up every Sunday night to invest in the members of the group and point them to Jesus. He also builds relationship with guys from his group throughout the week and is passionate about seeing them take ownership of their faith and surrender every area of their lives to God.

Here's what one of the guys said about Kyle: "Kyle was an outstanding mentor for me this year. He really helped me in my times of struggle or conflict and celebrated me in my times of victory and triumph. He was one of the people who prayed over my Bible study that was just starting up and it’s really helped me a lot. I’ll always remember how well he loved our core group and can’t thank him enough!"
