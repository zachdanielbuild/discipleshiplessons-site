Look Out · Lesson 2 of 4

Your Sphere of Influence

View on the current site
How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.

Introduction

As you get started, catch up on how y’all are doing since you last met. Celebrate how God has been at work in your lives. How did it go praying to be a witness and ambassador this last week?

Biblical Foundations

God has given us all a mission to be His witness and ambassadors. He has given each of us different spheres of influence where we live out that calling.

Acts 1:8 "But you will receive when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my in , and in all , and to the ."

Acts 17.26-27 And he made from one man every nation of mankind to live on all the face of the earth, having determined allotted and of their dwelling place, that they should seek God, and perhaps feel their way toward him and find him. Yet he is actually not far from each one of us

2 Corinthians 10.13 But we will not boast beyond limits, but will boast only with regard to the God to us, to even to you.

Questions for Discussion: What stands out to you about these verses? What are some “areas of influence” has God assigned to you?

Modeling

For the Leader: Share about your own areas of influence where God has called you to be an ambassador and a witness. What does this look like for you?

Together: Map out each of your areas of influence in this season:

  • Family
  • Friends
  • Neighbors
  • Co-workers/Classmates
  • Where do you eat?
  • Where do you play?
  • Other:

For each sphere, answer these questions:

  • Who in this sphere needs to hear about Jesus?
  • What unique opportunities do I have to be a witness in this sphere?
  • What obstacles might I face in being a witness here?
  • How can I be more intentional in representing Christ in this area?

Practice

Begin praying specifically for 3-5 people in your spheres of influence who don't know Jesus. Write their names here:
