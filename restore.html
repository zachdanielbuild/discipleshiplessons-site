Restore

1Connection2Vision3Biblical Foundations4Modeling5Practice

Restore · Look In · Lesson 5 of 5 · discipleshiplessons.com

How to use this lessonRead the Scriptures together and fill in the blanks. The leader shares their own story in Modeling. Then pick one thing to practice before you meet again.
1

Connection

As you get started, catch up on how y’all are doing since you last met. Celebrate how God has been at work in your lives. Using the Galatians 5 passage, take time to practice confession and repentance of sin. How did it go in uprooting these areas from your lives?

2

Vision

Everything God is doing in you is leading somewhere beautiful: a life of holiness and love that looks like Jesus. You were created for this. As He heals and transforms you, you’ll find yourself loving God and loving others more freely than ever.

Questions for Discussion:

  1. How do these verses inspire you in your relationship with the Lord?
  2. What are some of the marks of restoration that you see in these verses?
4

Modeling

For the Leader: Share your own experience with God's restoration. Describe how God has healed wounds, restored relationships, and given you purpose through the process of transformation.

5

Practice

As you close out the Look In lessons, go through our Freedom Prayer Experience together. This experience takes what you learned in these lessons and applies them to several common areas of the life of a believer to help you walk in greater freedom, holiness and love for others.
