2

Going Deeper in Looking Up

Here are some good questions and curated activities to building on the foundations that you laid in looking up and cultivating a lifestyle of knowing, loving and walking with the Lord.

Good Questions:

  • How are you doing in your devotional life?
  • What has the Lord been speaking to you, and how are you responding?
  • How are you abiding in Jesus throughout the day?
  • How can I/we spur you on in this area of discipleship?

Curated Activities:

  • Pick a book of the Bible to study as a group.
  • Practice through the Alone With God workbook and try a new tool from it each week.
  • Read Live Dead Joy or God is My Hiding Place devotional together.
  • Pray together in the House of Prayer
  • Practice through Tending Your Heart curriculum together.
  • Read Brother Lawrence’s book Practicing the Presence of God together and talk about abiding with God throughout the day.
  • Read Richard Foster’s Celebration of Discipline together and practice incorporating different spiritual disciplines in your life to help you abide in Christ.
3

Going Deeper in Looking In

Here are some good questions and curated activities to building on the foundations that you laid in looking in and cultivating a lifestyle of holiness, love and Christlike character:

Good Questions:

  • Is there any sin in your life that you need to confess and repent of?
  • What burden, need, challenges or big decision can I pray with you about?
  • How can we spur you on in holiness and love?

Curated Activities:

  • Use John Wesley’s list of 22 accountability questions as a primer to create your own accountability questions as a group.
  • Receive freedom prayer ministry
  • Memorize the Sermon on the Mount and/or Galatians 5
  • Read Bitter Free by Mark DeJesus together
  • Do Freedom Fight together.
4

Going Deeper in Looking Out

Here are some good questions and curated activities to building on the foundations that you laid in looking in and cultivating a Great Commission lifestyle:

Good Questions:

  • How are you being salt and light in your sphere of influence?
  • Who are you discipling (investing in), and how can I help you with that? Who are they investing in?
  • Who are you reaching out to that does not know Jesus, and how can I help you?

Curated Activities:

  • Do Roles & Goals together
  • Read Business Revival by Brandon Haire together
  • Read Passion and Purpose by Jimmy Seibert together
  • Serve in a disciple-making ministry team together.
  • Spend time praying for the disciple-making efforts of the group members.
  • Spend time praying by name for those who don’t know Jesus.
  • Set a goal as a group for sharing your faith.
  • Go on a mission trip together or serve in an evangelistic ministry together.
  • Start a Lifegroup or Discipleship group together
